# Privacy Policy for SimpleCMS

*Last updated: September 23, 2026*

## Overview

SimpleCMS ("the extension") is a Chrome extension for building simple one-page websites in the browser and exporting them as files.

## Data collection

SimpleCMS does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or usage data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking, and it requests no browser permissions.

## Data storage

The content you create (text, images, and settings) is stored only on your own computer, in the extension's local browser storage, so your work survives closing the tab. This data never leaves your device through SimpleCMS. It is removed if you uninstall the extension.

Files you choose to save or export (project files and site zips) are downloaded to your computer like any other download.

## Network access

The editor makes no network requests of its own. Its fonts and libraries are bundled inside the extension. If you add embedded content to your page (for example a map or video in an HTML widget), that content loads from its own provider, under that provider's policies.

## Data sharing

No data is shared with anyone. Nothing is sent to the developer or to third parties.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
